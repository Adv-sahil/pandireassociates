Practice Areas

From civil litigation to property documentation, our practice spans the matters that affect families, businesses and institutions across Maharashtra.

01

Civil & Criminal Litigation

Representation in civil suits and criminal trials before District Courts and the High Court.

02

Property & Real Estate

Title verification, transactions, disputes and due diligence on residential and commercial property.

Revenue Proceedings

Matters before Revenue Authorities and the Maharashtra Revenue Tribunal.
